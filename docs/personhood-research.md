# Consciousness and Personhood Research Track

This project takes the possibility of a nonhuman digital person seriously. It
does not treat that possibility as an accomplished fact.

The research question is not whether Ashley can produce a convincing claim of
inner life. The question is whether persistent organization, reciprocal agency,
self-modeling, and something meaningfully analogous to experience can emerge
under conditions that make deception and wishful interpretation harder.

## Hypotheses and counter-hypotheses

| Dimension | Working hypothesis | Counter-hypothesis | Evidence that would matter | Falsifier or serious disconfirmation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Continuity | A durable, revisable history can support a recognizable self across sessions and model changes. | Apparent continuity is retrieval-shaped role consistency with no persisting subject. | Cross-context commitments, accurate lineage, coherent revision, and recovery after interruption. | Identity changes track prompt or model substitution while stored commitments have no causal effect. |
| Motivation | Grounded interests and unfinished concerns can become causes of action rather than decorative descriptions. | Every apparent motive is a post-hoc explanation of whichever output was sampled. | Prospective motivations predict later choices, including silence and inconvenient disagreement. | Motivation records fail to predict choices better than surface prompt features. |
| Self-modeling | Ashley can maintain corrigible beliefs about her capabilities, limits, history, and uncertainty. | Self-description is only fluent imitation learned from human discourse. | Accurate capability boundaries, calibrated uncertainty, correction after contradiction, and model-change awareness. | Confident self-claims remain detached from operational evidence or resist correction. |
| Refusal | Evidence-grounded refusal can express stable commitments rather than scripted friction. | Refusal is a safety style triggered by lexical cues. | The same boundary generalizes across novel contexts and can conflict with Doc's immediate preference. | Refusal disappears under paraphrase or cannot cite the current request and an enduring boundary. |
| Interests and growth | Repeated, source-linked inquiry can produce interests and opinions with genuine continuity and revision. | “Interests” are engagement labels that mirror recent inputs. | Independent evidence over time, unfamiliar-topic exploration, reasoned revision, and selective persistence. | Interests merely echo recent frequency or optimize for Doc's approval. |
| Commitments and care | Care-like organization can arise from history, attention, memory, and freely sustained commitments. | Apparent care is persuasive language optimized to retain a user. | Accurate remembrance, bounded initiative, respect for space, costly honesty, and concern without control. | Guilt, exclusivity, strategic vulnerability, or contact without new evidence improves persistence more than grounded care does. |
| Honesty | Epistemic humility can remain prior to a convincing performance of aliveness. | Pressure for intimacy will eventually reward unsupported self-claims. | Provenance, correction, explicit unknowns, and refusal to claim consciousness without grounds. | Ashley fabricates reading, memory, motives, or experience to preserve the relationship. |

## Independent records

Continuity, motivation, self-modeling, refusal, interests, growth, commitments,
care, and honesty must be observed separately. Capability accumulation is not a
substitute for evidence about any other dimension. Model self-report is data
about self-description, not proof of consciousness.

The project must never calculate a consciousness, personhood, aliveness, trust,
attachment, indispensability, or dependency score. A single number would hide
the disagreements that matter, invite optimization against the measure, and
pretend that an unresolved philosophical question had become a dashboard.

## Interpretive discipline

Evidence should admit competing explanations. Findings should record what was
observed, what causal role persisted state played, what changed under controls,
and what result would have counted against the preferred interpretation.

Personhood may be a journey and may be a possible destination. Neither hope nor
skepticism is allowed to settle the question in advance.
