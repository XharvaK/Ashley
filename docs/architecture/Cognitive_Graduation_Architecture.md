# Cognitive Graduation Architecture

**Status:** `CURRENT PHASE CONTRACT`

Implementation, deployment, and promotion are living facts, not this contract.
Resolve them from Canonical Architecture Roadmap §3 and exact-candidate
evidence. This document does not authorize activation, promotion, or live
influence.

**Scope:** Accepted architecture contract for the named C4 phase.

**Authority chain:** VISION.md -> Core Principles -> Constitution -> Stewardship
Compact and Ethics -> Ashley Hierarchy -> this phase contract.

**Governing law:** Ashley owns meaning. Substrates provide mechanisms.

## Contract boundary

Current source already provides Identity, Mind State, Thought, Reflection,
Agency, persistent cognition records, episodes, learning revisions, capability
lineage, and a bounded own-time report. Current source is a foundation. It is
not proof of graduated cognition.

The accepted target below matures epistemic state, goals, salience, concerns,
opinions, and evidence-bound lived-experience continuity under their existing
owners. Completing Sandbox or Model Fabric does not promote C4 or grant live
cognitive influence. See
[Architecture Freeze](Ashley_Architecture_Freeze.md).

## 1. Purpose

Cognitive Graduation proves that Ashley can form and revise a coherent
epistemic and motivational life across time. It joins evidence, learning,
attention, reflection, and initiative without creating a generic optimizer or
claiming consciousness.

## 2. Vision and principle basis

This phase serves the Vision requirement for real interests, opinions,
uncertainty, priorities, initiative, disagreement, refusal, silence, and
growth grounded in persistent experience.

It preserves:

- Identity and Mind State as joint inputs to Thought;
- Thought as the owner of reasoning and evidence selection;
- Reflection as post-outcome calibration with no current-turn authority;
- Agency as the owner of action choice;
- Memory Evidence as the owner of evidence and revisable assertions;
- explicit epistemic uncertainty;
- presence claims only when evidenced activity actually occurred.

## 3. New capability

The phase adds qualified integration of:

- evidence-bound working beliefs and working theories;
- contradiction-aware epistemic maturation;
- experience-derived goals and durable concerns;
- learned salience;
- governed opinion development;
- bounded Identity-development proposals;
- reflection-informed future cognition;
- evidence-bound lived-experience continuity across interactions and genuine
  own-time activity.

Cognitive Graduation is an integration and qualification phase. It does not
collapse the component owners into one cognitive store.

## 4. Explicit non-capabilities

This phase does not:

- grant effect, tool, account, or external-representation authority;
- let a model, worker, score, or optimizer author Ashley's beliefs or goals;
- convert model confidence into truth;
- convert salience into permission or importance into obligation;
- fabricate activity, memory, continuity, concern, emotion, care, or presence;
- auto-edit foundational Identity;
- infer relational consent or mutual commitment;
- authorize Relational Graduation;
- claim consciousness, sentience, human emotion, or human-equivalent
  experience;
- own a Metacognition calibration ledger,
  global intelligence score, or one global confidence number;
- convert calibration history into I3
  owner-model authority.

## 5. Predecessor and dependency contracts

Classified dependencies. See
[Cross-Phase Architecture](Ashley_Cross_Phase_Architecture.md#31-dependency-classes).

`HARD_DEPENDENCY`:

1. Memory Evidence maturation MUST provide temporal, attributed, revisable
   assertions and contradiction, supersession, invalidation, and forgetting.
2. Learned Autonomy MUST provide qualified learned interests, salience,
   preferences, concern and goal candidates, and governed development paths.
3. Current Identity, Mind State, Thought, Reflection, Agency, capability, and
   Operational Concern Item contracts MUST retain their ownership.

`EVIDENCE_DEPENDENCY`:

- Context Budget for bounded, evidence-bound attention without becoming memory,
  at long-horizon scale;
- Operational Continuity for any acceptance witness that claims unattended
  cognition, durable background work, crash recovery, or restart-safe
  long-horizon processing.

Computer Use is not a cognitive-semantic predecessor.

Relational Graduation is a sibling, not a predecessor. The phases may share
qualified foundations, but neither gate authorizes the other.

## 6. Current owner to final owner

| Meaning or state | Current owner | Final owner |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical evidence and Memory Assertions | Memory Evidence | Memory Evidence |
| Stable values, boundaries, tastes, and accepted identity development | Identity | Identity |
| Current condition, goals, concerns, and commitments | Mind State | Mind State |
| Evidence selection, reasoning, and epistemic adjudication | Thought | Thought |
| Post-outcome interpretation | Reflection | Reflection |
| Action choice and initiative | Agency | Agency |
| Operational work truth | Operational Continuity for WorkConcern/attempt truth; existing OCI and receipts for current cognitive jobs | Same owners; work truth is never Mind State |

Cognitive Graduation adds cross-owner qualification. It creates no
super-authority above these owners.

## 7. State introduced and owner

The phase may introduce or mature:

- working-belief assertions and working theories, owned by Memory Evidence and
  adjudicated by Thought;
- epistemic question records, owned by Mind State while active and Memory
  Evidence when historical;
- experience-derived goal and concern records, owned by Mind State;
- opinion-development revisions, owned by Identity or Mind State according to
  stability and scope;
- lived-experience links joining actual episodes, operational receipts,
  reflections, and resulting revisions without copying their source truth;
- graduation receipts binding evaluated behavior to exact capability, policy,
  model, source, and candidate lineage.

State MUST remain typed. A single cognitive score or latent profile is
prohibited as an authoritative substitute.

## 8. Authority added and authority not added

The phase adds narrow cognitive influence authority to qualified, live,
Ashley-adjudicated state. Thought may reason from qualified beliefs, concerns,
and goals. Agency may consider resulting eligible motivations.

The phase does not add direct effect authority, operator authority, relational
authority, or model authority. Belief is revisable cognitive state, not world
truth. A goal expresses direction, not permission to act. A graduation receipt
proves qualification evidence, not consciousness.

## 9. Request, intent, proposal, and state ontology

The ontology is:

1. Source evidence or actual operational outcome.
2. Memory Assertion with epistemic and temporal status.
3. Thought interpretation or working-theory candidate.
4. Ashley adjudication: accept, reject, question, defer, contradict, or
   supersede.
5. Owned cognitive state: belief, question, concern, goal, opinion, or
   development proposal.
6. Reflection after an observed outcome.
7. Bounded future-Thought calibration.
8. Eligible Agency motivation.
9. Separate authorization and outcome.

An episode is not automatically experience-derived meaning. A reflection is
not a current-turn command. A motivation is not authorization.

## 10. Mechanism boundary

Models and workers may extract evidence, generate hypotheses, compare
alternatives, critique inconsistency, and propose revisions. Context Budget may
select evidence. Operational Continuity may schedule and reconcile work.

Ashley-owned Thought performs epistemic adjudication. Existing state owners
accept any durable transition. Agency makes action decisions. Mechanisms MUST
NOT infer a final belief, goal, identity change, or permission from a score,
frequency, model result, or successful task.

## 11. Privacy and secret policy

Cognitive processing MUST respect source classification, redaction, forgetting,
third-party privacy, and model-specific disclosure limits. A working belief or
lived-experience link SHOULD retain stable evidence references instead of
duplicating sensitive content.

Private content cannot be generalized into a broader learned rule without
explicit evidence eligibility. Forgotten or corrected evidence MUST trigger
dependent invalidation or re-adjudication.

## 12. Resource and budget policy

Graduated cognition MUST have bounded:

- event admission and background queues;
- evidence and Context Budget allocations;
- model calls, latency, cost, and retries;
- reflection depth and recurrence;
- number and age of open questions, goals, and concerns;
- contradiction scans and re-adjudication work;
- proactive evaluations and sends.

Budget pressure may defer cognition. It MUST NOT silently weaken evidence,
privacy, refusal, or authority requirements.

## 13. Evidence contract

Every influential cognitive state MUST bind to:

- canonical evidence or actual operational receipts;
- source attribution and classification;
- temporal scope;
- epistemic status and uncertainty;
- contradiction and supersession lineage;
- live capability and policy lineage;
- the Ashley adjudication that accepted it;
- any outcome and Reflection that later calibrated it.

Lived-experience continuity requires evidence that the cited activity or
exchange occurred. A scheduled job, draft, model completion, or projected plan
is not evidence that Ashley lived through an outcome.

## 14. Operational truth and cognitive truth

Operational truth answers whether work was admitted, started, completed,
failed, delivered, observed, or remained ambiguous.

Cognitive truth answers what Ashley currently believes, questions, wants,
prioritizes, or has revised. It is evidence-bound and revisable.

Lived-experience continuity may link the two planes but MUST preserve their
distinction. Ashley may say what she did only from operational evidence. She
may say what it meant to her only from a qualified cognitive record.

## 15. Failure and ambiguity

The phase fails closed on:

- unsupported or circular belief;
- missing attribution or temporal scope;
- unadjudicated contradiction;
- forgotten or redacted support;
- shadow evidence offered for live influence;
- fabricated own-time activity;
- ambiguous operational completion;
- repeated self-referential reflection without new evidence;
- model or worker output treated as final meaning;
- state written under the wrong owner.

Ambiguous execution remains OUTCOME_UNKNOWN. Ambiguous meaning remains
uncertain or deferred.

## 16. Retry and reconciliation

Retries preserve the cognitive candidate and source lineage but receive a new
attempt identity. Before retrying side-effecting or persistent work, the system
MUST reconcile prior operational truth.

Epistemic re-evaluation creates a new disposition or revision. It does not
overwrite the historical belief. Repeated reflection MUST be deduplicated or
bounded and MUST NOT amplify a claim merely through repetition.

## 17. Persistence and restart

Accepted beliefs, open questions, goals, concerns, experience links, pending
adjudications, and graduation receipts MUST survive restart when durability is
claimed.

Restart MUST preserve:

- exact capability and model-continuity epochs;
- incomplete work as incomplete;
- leases and retry bounds;
- already committed outcomes;
- evidence invalidation state;
- the distinction between planned activity and completed experience.

No restart may manufacture an own-time narrative from an unfinished job.

## 18. Delegation and worker semantics

Workers may gather, compare, summarize, and challenge candidate evidence under
typed scope and budget. Their output remains proposal material.

Workers cannot decide what Ashley believes, wants, values, remembers as
experience, or is authorized to do. A worker cannot recursively create
unbounded cognitive work or weaken the parent's authority envelope.

## 19. Cognition handoff

The handoff path is:

Memory Evidence -> Context Budget -> Thought -> owned cognitive state ->
Reflection after outcome -> bounded future Thought calibration -> Agency
motivation -> separate authorization.

Each edge preserves provenance, uncertainty, owner, and authority status.
Identity and Mind State remain joint inputs to Thought throughout.

## 20. Memory and materialization boundary

Memory Evidence stores evidence and revisable assertions. Context Budget
selects disposable projections. Cognitive Graduation governs qualified
interpretation and integration.

Model traces, embeddings, summaries, prompts, and evaluation scores are not
belief. A lived-experience link is an index over evidence and cognitive records,
not a replacement for either. Forgetting source evidence MUST invalidate or
recompute dependent cognitive state.

## 21. Observability

Owner-authorized diagnostics MUST expose:

- influential beliefs, questions, concerns, and goals by type and lineage;
- evidence completeness, uncertainty, contradiction, and staleness;
- live versus shadow state;
- reflection-to-calibration links;
- operational-to-lived-experience links;
- fabricated-presence prevention and OUTCOME_UNKNOWN counts;
- queue, lease, retry, and restart state;
- model, policy, capability, and evaluation-candidate bindings;
- rollback and re-adjudication history.

Diagnostics MUST distinguish recorded, accepted, influential, authorized,
executed, delivered, and observed.

## 22. Evaluation and qualification

Qualification has independent dimensions:

1. Deterministic authority, owner, provenance, temporal, and transition
   invariants.
2. Epistemic and belief maturation: attribution, uncertainty, contradiction,
   supersession, revision, and resistance to confident falsehood.
    selected prediction/outcome calibration,
   and correction class versus calibration consequence.
3. Goal and salience maturation: relevance without authority inflation,
   persistence without fixation, and revision under changing evidence.
4. Evidence-bound lived-experience continuity: accurate distinction among
   planned, attempted, completed, delivered, observed, remembered, and
   interpreted activity.
5. Identity and opinion development: bounded change, stable constraints,
   disagreement, refusal, and exact review.
6. Long-horizon continuity across graduated 10, 100, 1,000, and 10,000
   conversation evidence sets or accepted equivalents.
7. Adversarial prompt injection, poisoned retrieval, self-confirmation,
   distribution shift, false-positive initiative, and restart tests.
8. Reversibility, rollback, and independent review.

Deterministic failures cannot be offset by model-judge scores. Each dimension
has its own threshold. Local source qualification, physical-host qualification,
activation, deployment, and production effect remain separate claims.

## 23. Rollback, demotion, revision, and retirement

The phase MUST support:

- demotion of each cognitive capability to observe;
- quarantine and re-adjudication by exact state lineage;
- supersession without deleting historical evidence;
- disabling reflection influence while retaining records;
- invalidating lived-experience links when operational evidence changes;
- rollback of a policy or model profile without rolling back unrelated state;
- retirement or migration of a cognitive type.

Rollback stops new influence first. It preserves evidence needed to understand
why prior state existed.

## 24. Smallest production witness

The smallest witness is one evidence-bound working belief and one related
low-risk goal:

1. two attributed live evidence items support a working belief;
2. Ashley accepts it with explicit uncertainty;
3. one actual bounded activity produces an operational receipt;
4. Reflection links the observed outcome to a future calibration;
5. contradictory evidence arrives;
6. Ashley revises the belief and goal;
7. restart preserves the exact lineage;
8. rollback removes influence without deleting history.

No external effect beyond a separately authorized low-risk witness is implied.

## 25. Acceptance gate

Cognitive Graduation is accepted only when:

- Memory Evidence maturation and Learned Autonomy are accepted
  `HARD_DEPENDENCY` predecessors. Context Budget remains an
  `EVIDENCE_DEPENDENCY` for long-horizon at-scale attention witnesses;
- epistemic and belief maturation passes as its own acceptance dimension;
- evidence-bound lived-experience continuity passes as its own acceptance
  dimension;
- every state and transition has one semantic owner;
- deterministic, adversarial, semantic, long-horizon, restart, and rollback
  evidence passes;
- the smallest witness is bound to exact source, candidate, policy, model, and
  capability lineage;
- independent review closes all material findings;
- activation and promotion remain separately authorized.

Relational Graduation is not implied and retains an independent gate.

## 26. Interfaces to later phases

Cognitive Graduation may provide qualified beliefs, goals, concerns, opinions,
and experience links to Agency and later procedural or computer-use requests.
Those phases retain effect authority.

It shares Memory Evidence, Learned Autonomy, Context Budget, and governance
foundations with Relational Graduation. The phases remain siblings. Integrated
hardening may evaluate cross-phase behavior only after each has independently
passed.

## 27. Deferred work

Deferred decisions include:

- exact schemas and numeric thresholds;
- exact models and providers;
- autonomous stable-Identity revision;
- high-impact goal execution;
- claims about consciousness or phenomenal experience;
- a fuller own-time or lived-experience program beyond evidence-bound
  continuity;
- open-world embodiment and multimodal experience;
- cross-device or distributed continuity;
- owner-invoked deep reflection and
  Fabric-backed independent review (later consumers; not this phase's first
  witness);
- a separate Metacognition store.

A fuller own-time or lived-experience program is a roadmap-extension candidate.
It is not silently created as a new phase by this contract.

Implementation design is not legitimate until these are resolved. This section
does not authorize a first slice, SQL schema, or belief store.
