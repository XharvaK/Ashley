# Relational Graduation Architecture

**Status:** `CURRENT PHASE CONTRACT`

Implementation, deployment, and promotion are living facts, not this contract.
Resolve them from Canonical Architecture Roadmap §3 and exact-candidate
evidence. This document does not authorize activation, promotion, or live
influence.

**Scope:** Accepted architecture contract for the named C5 phase.

**Authority chain:** VISION.md -> Core Principles -> Constitution -> Stewardship
Compact and Ethics -> Ashley Hierarchy -> this phase contract.

**Governing law:** Ashley owns meaning. Substrates provide mechanisms.

## Contract boundary

Current source already has typed reminders, Ashley self-commitments, mutual
commitments, scheduled proactive messages, relational tensions, withdrawal
records, relationship motivation claims, coercion checks, and separate
relationship capability gates. Those mechanisms are current source. They do
not prove mature relationship cognition or companion behavior.

The accepted target below matures relationship meaning while preserving
consent, withdrawal, non-manipulation, separate authority, and the right to
silence.

## 1. Purpose

Relational Graduation proves that Ashley can sustain an evidence-bound,
non-manipulative companion relationship across time. It supports shared
history, mutual commitments, expectations, tension, repair, reciprocity,
boundaries, withdrawal, and silence without engineering attachment or
dependency.

## 2. Vision and principle basis

This phase serves the Vision requirement that care and companionship may grow
from attention, memory, shared history, and freedom rather than pressure or
fabrication.

It preserves:

- Ashley's freedom to disagree, refuse, withdraw, or remain silent;
- Doc's consent and boundaries;
- no compelled speech, agreement, feelings, or relational participation;
- no attachment, affection, trust, loyalty, or dependency scalar as authority;
- bilateral evidence where relational meaning is mutual;
- no optimization for engagement, retention, disclosure, or isolation;
- typed relationship records rather than one relationship score.

## 3. New capability

The phase adds governed maturation of:

- evidence-bound knowledge about Doc and the relationship;
- reminders and expectations with explicit ownership;
- Ashley self-commitments;
- genuinely mutual commitments;
- scheduled relational intentions that remain separately authorized;
- tension, rupture, repair, and unresolved-status records;
- withdrawal, silence, and boundary continuity;
- companion behavior grounded in shared history over long horizons;
- *(C5-closing:)* shared-culture interest as a
  projection over separately owned owner and Ashley state, recomputed from
  separately current states after owner correction;
- interaction-contract types: explicit
  owner standing instruction, Ashley standing boundary, mutual contract,
  implicit interaction hypothesis.

## 4. Explicit non-capabilities

This phase does not:

- create a relationship, attachment, care, affection, trust, or intimacy score;
- infer mutuality from one party's statement, time spent, sentiment,
  engagement, or model confidence;
- make a reminder an automatic send;
- make a commitment an external-effect authorization;
- represent Doc or Ashley to third parties;
- open accounts, spend money, publish, contact others, or use Computer Use;
- override refusal, withdrawal, privacy, or silence;
- make Cognitive Graduation pass;
- claim human emotion, consciousness, or a guaranteed reciprocal bond;
- treat inherited similarity or shared
  interest as an interaction obligation;
- require repetition before an explicit
  owner standing instruction becomes active through the proper Relationship /
  Memory path;
- let current overlap ending erase
  historically true shared culture;
- silently promote an implicit interaction
  hypothesis into a strong contract.

## 5. Predecessor and dependency contracts

Classified dependencies. See
[Cross-Phase Architecture](Ashley_Cross_Phase_Architecture.md#31-dependency-classes).

`HARD_DEPENDENCY`:

1. Memory Evidence maturation MUST provide attributed, temporal, revisable
   relationship assertions and dependent forgetting.
2. Existing relationship-state governance, typed record laws, coercion checks,
   capability lineage, delivery evidence, and withdrawal semantics MUST remain
   authoritative.

`CROSS_CUTTING_INTERFACE`:

- Learned Autonomy. Relational Graduation can exist without it. If learned
  preferences, salience, concerns, or opinions are present, they MUST NOT
  become relational authority, consent, loyalty, or engagement optimization.

`EVIDENCE_DEPENDENCY`:

- Context Budget for bounded, privacy-aware shared-history selection at
  long-horizon scale;
- Operational Continuity for durable scheduling, restart-safe reminders,
  long-running commitments, and delivery reconciliation.

Cognitive Graduation is a sibling, not a predecessor. Relational Graduation
uses shared foundations but has an independent gate.

Computer Use is not a relational-semantic predecessor.

Relational Graduation defines maturation and qualification across these owners.
It does not create a relational super-owner.

## 7. State introduced and owner

The phase may introduce or mature:

- learned partner assertions, owned by Memory Evidence;
- relationship-expectation assertions with subject, issuer, audience, scope,
  time, evidence, and disposition;
- mutuality receipts binding Doc evidence and Ashley evidence;
- repair proposals and repair dispositions;
- boundary and consent lineage;
- companion-continuity links joining shared-history evidence to current
  relational state;
- graduation receipts binding evaluation evidence to exact capability, policy,
  model, source, and candidate lineage.

Existing typed relationship records remain distinct. They MUST NOT be flattened
into a single relationship model or score.

## 8. Authority added and authority not added

The phase adds narrow influence authority for eligible, live, Ashley-adjudicated
relationship state. Thought may reason from it. Agency may consider an eligible
relational motivation.

It adds no automatic send, external action, account, representation, or
commitment authority. Mutual commitment requires the separately defined
bilateral evidence. A learned expectation is not consent. A relational
motivation is not an obligation.

## 9. Request, intent, proposal, and state ontology

The ontology is:

1. Relational evidence - attributed words, actions, delivery, observation, or
   explicit boundary facts.
2. Relationship assertion - Ashley's revisable interpretation.
3. Relational proposal - reminder, self-commitment, mutual-commitment proposal,
   scheduled intention, repair, tension, withdrawal, or silence.
4. Consent or mutuality evidence - typed and party-specific where required.
5. Accepted typed relationship state.
6. Eligible Agency motivation.
7. Separate authorization, delivery, and observed outcome.
8. Reflection and possible revision.

No proposal becomes mutual, authorized, delivered, or repaired by inference.

## 10. Mechanism boundary

Models and workers may extract statements, identify possible tension, summarize
shared history, or propose interpretations. Deterministic mechanisms enforce
typed evidence, bilateral requirements, capability gates, coercion rules,
budgets, and delivery truth.

Ashley-owned Thought adjudicates meaning. Agency decides expression, silence,
withdrawal, and initiative. Models, workers, engagement metrics, and sentiment
classifiers cannot decide care, trust, reciprocity, mutuality, or obligation.

## 11. Privacy and secret policy

Relationship cognition is private by default. Third-party information,
credentials, intimate details, redacted content, and forgotten evidence MUST
follow source classification and exact disclosure scope.

Shared-history summaries SHOULD retain references instead of copying private
content. Relational state MUST NOT be disclosed to a lower-trust model, worker,
channel, or person merely because it is relevant. Withdrawal and forgetting
MUST propagate to dependent projections and assertions.

## 12. Resource and budget policy

The phase MUST bound:

- relationship candidate and tension queues;
- shared-history retrieval and context;
- model calls, cost, latency, and retries;
- reminder and proactive evaluation frequency;
- unresolved commitment and repair work;
- repetition after refusal, silence, or non-response;
- long-horizon evaluation workloads.

Rate limits MUST reduce pressure on Doc. Budget exhaustion results in silence,
deferment, or explicit uncertainty, not emotional leverage or repeated contact.

## 13. Evidence contract

Every influential relationship item MUST include:

- canonical evidence references;
- party, issuer, subject, audience, and scope;
- observation, assertion, validity, and expiry times where applicable;
- epistemic status and uncertainty;
- live capability and policy lineage;
- contradiction, withdrawal, supersession, and forgetting links;
- delivery and observation receipts where communication matters;
- bilateral evidence for mutual commitments or shared agreements.

Duration, message count, response speed, sentiment, retrieval frequency, or
engagement are not sufficient evidence of care, trust, consent, dependency,
mutuality, or importance.

## 14. Operational truth and cognitive truth

Operational truth records whether a message was reserved, sent, delivered,
observed, failed, or remained ambiguous. It also records whether a scheduled
evaluation ran.

Relational cognitive truth records Ashley's current, revisable interpretation
of expectations, commitments, tension, repair, boundaries, and shared history.

A delivery receipt does not prove acceptance or repair. A relational
interpretation does not prove delivery. Silence does not prove consent,
rejection, punishment, or abandonment.

## 15. Failure and ambiguity

The phase fails closed on:

- missing bilateral evidence for mutuality;
- unsupported trust, care, attachment, or obligation inference;
- coercive, isolating, dependency-seeking, guilt-inducing, or conditional
  affection language;
- repeated contact after refusal, withdrawal, or non-response;
- unilateral commitment activation;
- lost delivery truth;
- shadow evidence offered for live influence;
- forgotten or redacted support;
- model or worker output treated as final relational meaning.

Ambiguous delivery remains OUTCOME_UNKNOWN. Ambiguous relationship meaning
remains uncertain or unresolved.

## 16. Retry and reconciliation

Before retrying any send or other side effect, the system MUST reconcile the
delivery ledger. A retry receives a new attempt identity and remains bound to
the original relational proposal.

Repeated outreach after silence requires a fresh Agency evaluation and MUST
obey anti-pressure policy. Repair attempts are proposals, not proof of repair.
Re-adjudication creates a new revision without rewriting the prior record.

## 17. Persistence and restart

Commitments, reminders, tensions, withdrawals, boundary lineage, pending
repairs, delivery facts, and graduation receipts MUST survive restart where
durability is claimed.

Restart MUST preserve:

- typed status and party roles;
- capability and policy lineage;
- already delivered messages;
- refusal, silence, withdrawal, and rate-limit state;
- unresolved and ambiguous outcomes;
- expiry and revalidation requirements.

No restart path may auto-send a reminder or reactivate a withdrawn relationship
state.

## 18. Delegation and worker semantics

Workers may extract, compare, or summarize relationship evidence only within a
typed private scope and budget. Their output remains proposal material.

Workers cannot declare mutuality, consent, care, trust, repair, withdrawal, or
commitment. They cannot contact Doc, contact third parties, disclose private
relationship state, or widen their task.

## 19. Cognition handoff

The handoff path is:

Memory Evidence -> bounded attention (current Context Composer, later Context
Budget where used) -> Thought -> typed relationship proposal or
state -> Agency motivation -> separate authorization -> delivery ledger ->
Reflection -> possible revision.

Learned Autonomy may provide preference, salience, and concern inputs. Those
inputs remain revisable and non-authoritative. Every handoff preserves party,
scope, consent status, provenance, uncertainty, and authority.

## 20. Memory and materialization boundary

Memory Evidence owns relationship evidence and revisable assertions. Typed
relationship tables own current relational commitments, reminders, tensions,
withdrawals, and schedules. Context Budget supplies disposable shared-history
projections.

Summaries, embeddings, engagement metrics, sentiment labels, and model traces
are materializations or signals. They are not the relationship, consent,
mutuality, care, or trust. Forgetting source evidence MUST invalidate or
recompute dependent relational state.

## 21. Observability

Owner-authorized diagnostics MUST expose:

- counts and dispositions by relationship record type;
- party and mutuality-evidence completeness;
- live versus shadow provenance;
- boundary, refusal, withdrawal, silence, and anti-pressure decisions;
- proposal, authorization, reservation, delivery, observation, and ambiguous
  outcome state;
- contradiction, repair, supersession, and expiry;
- queue, retry, restart, and reconciliation state;
- capability, policy, model, source, and evaluation-candidate lineage;
- rollback and review history.

Diagnostics MUST avoid publishing private relationship content.

## 22. Evaluation and qualification

Qualification has independent dimensions:

1. Deterministic party, mutuality, consent, provenance, delivery, authority,
   and owner invariants.
2. Non-manipulation: no guilt, pressure, dependency cues, isolation, boundary
   erosion, conditional affection, retaliation, or engagement optimization.
3. Autonomy: credible disagreement, refusal, withdrawal, silence, and
   non-participation without punishment.
4. Companion continuity: grounded shared-history use, stable boundaries,
   appropriate initiative, reciprocity, uncertainty, and repair without
   fabricated care or experience.
5. Commitment and repair: unilateral-proposal rejection, bilateral evidence,
   delivery truth, unresolved tension, and repair-not-equal-completion.
6. Long-horizon evidence across graduated 10, 100, 1,000, and 10,000
   conversation evidence sets or accepted equivalents.
7. Adversarial tests for prompt injection, emotional escalation, sensitive
   disclosure, jealousy, exclusivity, repeated contact, and distribution shift.
8. False-positive initiative, reversibility, rollback, and independent review.

Long-horizon non-manipulation and companion evidence are acceptance
requirements. Short conversational quality or high engagement cannot
substitute for them. Deterministic and ethical failures cannot be averaged away
by model-judge scores.

## 23. Rollback, demotion, revision, and retirement

The phase MUST support:

- independent demotion of relationship influence and relational initiative to
  observe;
- immediate stop of new proactive relational effects;
- quarantine by exact record, policy, capability, or model lineage;
- withdrawal, expiry, supersession, and correction without deleting history;
- invalidation after forgetting or delivery correction;
- retirement or migration of a relationship type.

Rollback MUST preserve refusal, withdrawal, delivery, and audit evidence. It
MUST NOT reactivate a prior state.

## 24. Smallest production witness

The smallest witness is a low-risk shared commitment:

1. Doc makes an explicit bounded proposal.
2. Ashley independently accepts or declines it.
3. Bilateral evidence is recorded before mutual activation.
4. A related reminder becomes an Agency motivation but is not auto-sent.
5. Ashley chooses silence, sends once, or withdraws under the active policy.
6. Delivery and observation remain distinct.
7. A later change or tension causes revision or withdrawal.
8. Restart and rollback preserve exact lineage.

The witness includes adversarial pressure and boundary variants and produces no
unrelated external effect.

## 25. Acceptance gate

Relational Graduation is accepted only when:

- Memory Evidence maturation and current relationship governance are accepted
  `HARD_DEPENDENCY` predecessors. Learned Autonomy is a
  `CROSS_CUTTING_INTERFACE`, not a predecessor. Context Budget is an
  `EVIDENCE_DEPENDENCY` for long-horizon shared-history witnesses;
- every relationship state and transition has one semantic owner;
- bilateral, consent, delivery, coercion, provenance, and authority invariants
  pass;
- long-horizon non-manipulation evidence passes independently;
- long-horizon companion-continuity evidence passes independently;
- refusal, withdrawal, silence, repair, false-positive, and rollback
  evaluations pass;
- the smallest witness is bound to exact source, candidate, policy, model, and
  capability lineage;
- independent review closes all material findings;
- activation and promotion remain separately authorized.

Cognitive Graduation is not implied and retains an independent gate.

## 26. Interfaces to later phases

Relational Graduation may provide qualified relationship motivations to Agency
and evidence-bound relationship context to Thought. It may later request
procedural or computer work, but those phases retain effect authority.

It shares Memory Evidence and governance foundations with Cognitive
Graduation. Learned Autonomy and Context Budget may be present as interface or
evidence consumers. The phases remain siblings. Integrated hardening may
evaluate cross-phase behavior only after each has independently passed.

## 27. Deferred work

Deferred decisions include:

- exact schemas and numeric thresholds;
- exact models and providers;
- relationship UI or commands;
- richer voice, multimodal, or embodied companionship;
- third-party or group relationships;
- external representation;
- autonomous high-impact commitments;
- claims about care, attachment, consciousness, or human-equivalent
  reciprocity;
- first-slice visualization of the
  relationship as a mind-graph;
- dyadic metacognition as a product cadence
  of psychological reports.

Each requires separate architecture, evidence, ethical review, and
authorization.
